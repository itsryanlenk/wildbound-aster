# Player guide

Choose a ranger name and one of the three starting companions, then complete the first capture lesson. Approach visible wild creatures to encounter them. Battles are real time: move, read the enemy warning, dodge, and use the four equipped attacks as their cooldowns allow.

## Capture and care

Reduce a wild creature's health and choose a capture prism. The interface shows the chance for the current conditions. Carry supplies, switch companions, and use the healing cottage between encounters. A party holds six creatures; additional captures go to reserve storage. Use the field guide to inspect types, habitats, attacks and evolution information.

## Growth

In this build, every healthy party companion receives the full XP reward from a defeated enemy or successful capture. Fainted companions and reserves receive none. A newly caught healthy creature placed in the party can receive that capture reward too.

Growth Candy raises its selected healthy companion one level outside battle. Invalid targets or a capped companion do not consume Candy. Evolution follows the threshold shown in the guide, updates available legal moves and restores health. Level 60 is the cap.

## Journey

Restore the eight regional beacons, progress to the First Beacon, and keep collecting after the story. Regional travel and badge progression are part of the adventure. The reserve and field-guide filters help find missing species. Settings offers sound mixing, reduced motion, story replay and save backups.

## Accessibility and layout

Keyboard, mouse and touch controls are included. Portrait and landscape screens arrange controls differently; browser zoom and unusual device sizes may still need testing. Menus pause the world. Avoid relying on audio alone: warning and reward information is also presented visually. The game is not claimed to meet a formal accessibility certification.
